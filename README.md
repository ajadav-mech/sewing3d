# Sewing3D: 3D-Printed Products from CAD to Customer

**Co-Founder & Prototype Developer · Jun 2023 – May 2025 · India**

## English

### Summary
Co-founded a small 3D-printing business and took **20+ aircraft models** from idea to product, selling them through **Amazon India**. I handled design, prototyping, production and keeping the printer fleet running.

### What I did
- **Design**: modelled products in Fusion 360, SolidWorks and Blender, designed for printability (supports, orientation, assembly)
- **Prototyping**: iterated designs until they printed reliably and looked right
- **Production**: ran FDM and DLP printers (Bambu Lab, Creality, Anycubic) for batch production
- **Process improvement**: optimised production workflows and print settings to cut failures and time per part
- **Machine reliability**: maintenance, calibration and troubleshooting of the printer fleet

### Skills shown
`product development` `DfAM` `FDM` `DLP/resin` `Fusion 360` `SolidWorks` `Blender` `production planning` `process optimisation` `e-commerce`
